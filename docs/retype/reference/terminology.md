# Terminology

These terms describe the project at a high level. Their exact implementation may evolve.

## Source material

Material supplied to VySol to establish or expand a fictional world.

## World

The structured representation of a fictional setting and the information needed to reason about it over time.

## Entity

A meaningful thing in the world, such as a character, place, group, object, event, or other concept that benefits from being represented explicitly.

## Relationship

A meaningful connection between entities. A relationship should capture useful context about how or why the entities are connected.

## Retrieval

The process of selecting relevant world and source context for a task.

## Source-grounded information

Information directly supported by source material.

## Inference

A conclusion derived from available information without being directly stated by the source.

## Simulation state

Information created or changed as the world continues beyond its source material during roleplay or simulation.

## Character perspective

The subset and interpretation of world information available to a particular character at a particular point in time.
