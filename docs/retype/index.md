# VySol

VySol is an AI-assisted system for understanding fictional worlds and using that understanding to support grounded roleplay and simulation.

Instead of treating a world as a flat pile of context, VySol is built around preserving the things that make stories coherent over time: characters, relationships, places, events, knowledge, memory, and the difference between what is sourced, inferred, generated, or changed during simulation.

## What VySol is for

VySol is intended for fictional settings where continuity matters. Source material and world data become structured context that can help AI systems reason about what exists in the world, what happened, who knows what, and how those facts change over time.

The long-term goal is richer roleplay and simulation where characters and worlds remain understandable beyond a single chat window.

## Core ideas

### Source grounded

Canonical or source-supported information should remain distinguishable from inference, generated material, and simulation state.

### World understanding

Characters, relationships, locations, events, knowledge, memory, and story flow are connected parts of a world rather than isolated text fragments.

### Character perspective

A character's behavior should depend on what that character can reasonably know, remember, perceive, and believe at that point in the world.

### Continuity over time

VySol is designed around worlds that can change while retaining enough history to explain how they reached their current state.

## What VySol is not

VySol is not intended to be a generic chatbot, a simple retrieval demo, a wiki, or a system that only produces prose. Retrieval, structured world knowledge, memory, and simulation exist to support a coherent living world.

## Project status

VySol is under active development. The implementation will change as systems are tested and refined. This documentation describes delivered project behavior. Plans and experiments should not be treated as finished features.
